COLD CURTAIN · 02/10 · THE PROBLEM

Projected 40-year US wildfire losses: $5–36 trillion.

Losses are escalating year over year, concentrated at a known set of wildland–urban edges. January 2025 alone: $250B+ in economic loss and ~30 lives — over 300 lives in these corridors since 2017.

SOURCES: VERISK · ACCUWEATHER · NFPA · US JOINT ECONOMIC COMMITTEE · CAL FIRE03/10 →
COLD CURTAIN · 03/10 · THE PROPOSAL

Fixed suppression infrastructure at the point of attack.

Every commercial building in America is required to have a sprinkler system. No equivalent exists at community scale. This is that system: buried liquid-CO2 lines, automated nozzles, sited where the fires historically arrive.

Grid cells cap fire size — maximum burn area becomes a design parameter
Every head self-triggers thermally and is remotely addressable — works with the controller dead

COLD CURTAIN · 04/10 · DEPLOYMENT

Three continuous lines. Highest-risk segments first.

The end state is not scattered rings — it is continuous defensive lines along the fronts where the fires originate: the North Bay Line, the Sierra Foothill Line, and the Southern Front. Phases 1–3 build their highest expected-loss segments; later phases close the gaps.

COLD CURTAIN · 05/10 · CASE STUDY — ALTADENA, JANUARY 7, 2025

Run that night again.

Ignition in Eaton Canyon, 6:18 PM, 100-mph Santa Ana winds. The fire came out of the canyon mouth and took 9,000+ structures and 18 lives — most of them west of Lake Avenue, where evacuation orders arrived hours late. The mountain front where it happened is a Phase-1 curtain alignment.

COLD CURTAIN · 06/10 · THE AGENT

A captured byproduct, not a chemical.

The CO2 is already being produced — new gas-fired data-center campuses will vent millions of tonnes a year. Captured and staged, it becomes suppression capacity. To our knowledge, this use has never been proposed.

CHEAPEST SUPPLY: STREAMS ALREADY SEPARATED & COMPRESSED (GAS-PROCESSING) · TRUCKED, NOT PIPED · NO SEQUESTRATION-CREDIT CLAIM07/10 →
COLD CURTAIN · 07/10 · COMPATIBILITY
