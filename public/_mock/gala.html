The Evening
A single night that keeps a river alive for another generation.

For fourteen years the Riverside Conservancy has restored the wetlands, returned the otters, and reopened the banks to the people who love them. The Lumière Gala is the night we gather to ensure the next fourteen — over dinner, music, and an auction of once-in-a-lifetime lots, every penny of which flows back to the water.
