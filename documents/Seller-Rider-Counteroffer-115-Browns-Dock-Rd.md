**Buyer:** Nicola Lamberti and Sarah Lamberti ("**Buyer**")

**Base Contract:** That certain Statewide New Jersey REALTORS® Standard Form of Real Estate Sales Contract (Form 118‑Statewide | 07/2025.2) for the Property, executed by Buyer on June 26, 2026, together with all addenda thereto (collectively, the "**Contract**").

This Rider is Seller's counter‑offer to the Contract. This Rider is incorporated into and made a part of the Contract. **In the event of any conflict, inconsistency, or ambiguity between the terms of this Rider and the pre‑printed provisions of the Contract, the entries on the form, the Additional Contractual Provisions in Section 43, or any prior addendum or rider, the terms of this Rider shall govern and control.** All terms of the Contract not expressly modified by this Rider remain in full force and effect. To the extent this Rider amends the Contract, it amends Section 40 (Entire Agreement) accordingly, and the parties intend this Rider to be a written amendment signed by Buyer and Seller within the meaning of that Section.

## 4. Occupancy Holdback; Seller's Post‑Closing Possession

Notwithstanding Section 12 of the Contract (which otherwise gives possession to Buyer at Closing), the parties agree as follows:

4.1 **Occupancy Holdback.** At Closing, the sum of **$[HOLDBACK AMOUNT]** (the "**Occupancy Holdback**") shall be withheld from the net sale proceeds otherwise payable to Seller and shall be retained by the Escrowee in escrow to secure Seller's obligations under this Section 4. The Occupancy Holdback is in addition to, and separate from, the Deposit.

4.2 **No‑Cost Occupancy Period.** Seller may remain in exclusive possession and occupancy of the Property after Closing for a period of **thirty (30) days** following the Closing date (the "**No‑Cost Occupancy Period**") at **no use‑and‑occupancy charge** to Seller.

4.3 **Closing‑Delay Offset.** If the Closing is adjourned or delayed beyond the scheduled Closing date by reason of Seller's act or omission, then each day of such Seller‑caused delay **in excess of [thirty (30)] days** shall reduce the No‑Cost Occupancy Period on a day‑for‑day basis. Delay attributable to Buyer, to Buyer's lender, or to causes beyond Seller's reasonable control shall not reduce the No‑Cost Occupancy Period. *(See Drafting Note 4 — confirm the threshold and how the scheduled Closing date is fixed.)*

4.4 **Use‑and‑Occupancy Charge After the No‑Cost Period (Rent‑Back).** If Seller remains in possession after the No‑Cost Occupancy Period expires, Seller shall pay a daily use‑and‑occupancy charge (the "**Daily Charge**") that escalates over time, accruing for each day (or part of a day) of continued possession, as follows:

| Period of continued possession after the No‑Cost Occupancy Period | Daily Charge |
|---|---|
| Days 1 – [30] | $[__]/day |
| Days [31] – [60] | $[__]/day |
| Day [61] and thereafter | $[__]/day |

The Daily Charge shall be drawn down from, and paid to Buyer out of, the Occupancy Holdback by the Escrowee as it accrues.

4.5 **Draw‑Down, Surrender, and Release.** The Escrowee shall deduct accrued Daily Charges (and any amount due under Section 4.6 or 4.7) from the Occupancy Holdback and remit them to Buyer [monthly and upon surrender]. Upon Seller's surrender of possession in the condition required by Section 4.8 and completion of a satisfactory final walk‑through, the Escrowee shall release the remaining balance of the Occupancy Holdback to Seller within **[10]** days, less any accrued and unpaid Daily Charges and the documented cost of any damage beyond ordinary wear and tear.

4.6 **Outside Surrender Date; Holdback Exhaustion.** Seller shall vacate and surrender the Property on or before the earlier of (a) **[OUTSIDE SURRENDER DATE]**, and (b) the date on which the accrued Daily Charges would fully exhaust the Occupancy Holdback (the earlier date, the "**Surrender Deadline**"). *(See Drafting Note 5 — a hard outside date is recommended so Buyer is not left without security once the Holdback is consumed.)*

4.7 **Holdover.** If Seller remains in possession after the Surrender Deadline, Seller shall be a holdover occupant and shall pay use and occupancy at **$[HOLDOVER RATE]** per day (or **[2x]** the last applicable Daily Charge, whichever is greater), without limiting any other remedy available to Buyer. Seller shall indemnify and hold Buyer harmless from all losses, costs, and reasonable attorneys' fees arising from any holdover.

4.8 **Condition at Surrender.** Seller shall surrender the Property in "broom‑clean" condition, free of debris and of Seller's personal property, and in substantially the same condition as at Closing, ordinary wear and tear excepted, consistent with Section 23 of the Contract. The initial and final walk‑throughs under Section 25 of the Contract shall be conducted with respect to the date of Seller's surrender of possession rather than the Closing date.

4.9 **Utilities, Insurance, Maintenance, and Risk During Occupancy.** During Seller's post‑Closing occupancy: (a) Seller shall pay all utilities and shall maintain the interior and grounds in the condition required by Section 4.8; (b) Seller shall keep in force tenant/occupant liability and personal‑property (contents) insurance, and Buyer shall keep in force owner's hazard and liability insurance; (c) Seller shall be responsible for loss or damage to the Property caused by Seller or Seller's guests or invitees during the occupancy, beyond ordinary wear and tear; and (d) Seller assumes all risk to Seller's own personal property remaining at the Property.

4.10 **No Tenancy.** Seller's post‑Closing occupancy is a temporary, permissive license/use‑and‑occupancy arrangement for the limited period described above and **does not create a landlord‑tenant relationship or any tenancy.** Seller acknowledges that possession is permissive and time‑limited, agrees to surrender possession on the Surrender Deadline, and, to the fullest extent permitted by law, waives any notice or other protections that would otherwise be afforded to a residential tenant. *(See Drafting Note 3.)*

4.11 **Survival.** This Section 4 survives Closing and delivery of the deed.

## 5. As‑Is Sale; Waiver of Inspection and Certificate‑of‑Occupancy Contingencies; Informational Inspections

5.1 **As‑Is.** The Property is sold strictly **"AS IS, WHERE IS," with all faults.** Buyer has had full opportunity to examine the Property and relies solely on its own examination and judgment as to its condition and value, and not on any representation of Seller or the Brokers except as expressly set forth in the Contract. Seller shall have no obligation to make any repair, improvement, credit, allowance, or price reduction on account of the condition of the Property.

5.2 **Waiver of Inspection Contingency.** Buyer waives in their entirety the inspection contingency provisions of Section 16 of the Contract and any related structural, mechanical, radon, wood‑destroying‑insect, environmental, or other physical‑condition contingency. No condition revealed by any inspection or evaluation shall entitle Buyer to terminate the Contract, to require any repair, credit, or price adjustment, to renegotiate, or to the return of the Deposit or any escrow.

5.3 **Certificate of Occupancy / Municipal Approvals.** Consistent with Section 43 of the Contract, Buyer accepts sole responsibility for, and the cost of, obtaining any certificate of occupancy, continued‑certificate‑of‑occupancy, resale or change‑of‑occupancy inspection, smoke/carbon‑monoxide/fire‑extinguisher certification, or other municipal approval required for the transfer or occupancy of the Property. Notwithstanding Section 9 of the Contract, none of the foregoing is a contingency, and Seller shall have no obligation to obtain the same or to make or pay for any repair required in connection therewith. Because Seller will remain in occupancy after Closing under Section 4, any change‑of‑occupancy inspection or certificate shall be obtained in connection with Seller's surrender of possession. *(See Drafting Note 6.)*

5.4 **No Lead‑Based‑Paint Inspection Contingency.** Buyer acknowledges prior receipt of the EPA pamphlet "Protect Your Family From Lead In Your Home," receipt of Seller's lead‑based‑paint disclosure, and execution of the Lead‑Based Paint Disclosure addendum to the Contract. Buyer **waives the ten (10)‑day opportunity to conduct a lead‑based‑paint inspection and/or risk assessment** under Section 13(C) of the Contract and accepts the Property as‑is with respect to lead‑based paint and lead‑based‑paint hazards. *(This waives only the inspection opportunity, not Seller's underlying disclosure — see Drafting Note 1.)*

5.5 **Seller's Reaffirmation of Disclosure.** Seller reaffirms that the Seller's Property Condition Disclosure Statement previously furnished to Buyer remains, to the best of Seller's actual knowledge as of the date of this Rider, true and complete, and that Seller has disclosed the condition of the Property to the best of Seller's knowledge.

5.6 **Informational Inspections and Walk‑Throughs — Seller's Prior Written Approval Required.** Buyer may from time to time request inspections, assessments, surveys, or architectural, engineering, contractor, subcontractor, or similar walk‑throughs or activities at the Property. **Each such activity requires Seller's prior written approval, which Seller may grant or withhold in Seller's sole and absolute discretion as to both the appointment (timing) and the scope of the activity.** Seller shall use best efforts to accommodate Buyer's reasonable requests. Buyer shall schedule all such activities through Seller, shall conduct them so as to minimize disruption to Seller's occupancy, shall promptly repair and restore any disturbance, shall ensure that each third‑party vendor carries adequate liability insurance, and shall indemnify and hold Seller harmless from any loss, injury, lien, or damage arising from such activities.

5.7 **Informational Purposes Only.** Any Seller‑approved activity under Section 5.6 is for Buyer's **informational purposes only.** Regardless of its findings or results, no such activity shall: (a) constitute, create, or revive any contingency; (b) give Buyer any right to terminate, adjourn, or renegotiate the Contract; (c) entitle Buyer to any repair, credit, allowance, or price adjustment; or (d) affect, delay, jeopardize, or entitle Buyer to the return of the Deposit or any escrow. Buyer's obligation to complete the purchase is absolute and unconditional with respect to the condition of the Property.

5.8 **Displacement Counts Toward Seller's Occupancy Credit.** If any Seller‑approved activity under Section 5.6 requires Seller to vacate or be displaced from the Property for any period of time, then each calendar day on which any such displacement occurs shall **extend the No‑Cost Occupancy Period by one (1) full day** (or, if the No‑Cost Occupancy Period has already expired, shall credit Seller one (1) Daily Charge against the Occupancy Holdback), **regardless of the duration of the displacement on that day** — any displacement during a day, however brief, counts as one full day.

5.9 **Survival.** Sections 5.1 through 5.5 survive Closing and delivery of the deed; nothing in this Section 5 modifies any disclosure obligation of Seller that survives the Closing as a matter of law.

## 6. Financing Is Not a Contingency

6.1 **No Mortgage Contingency.** Buyer's obligations under the Contract are **not contingent upon Buyer obtaining any mortgage, loan, or other financing.** The mortgage‑contingency provisions of Section 3(D) of the Contract are waived and deleted in their entirety.

6.2 **Buyer May Use Financing.** Buyer may, at its election, obtain financing, provided that Buyer's use of financing shall not delay the Closing, alter any date or term of the Contract, or create any condition or contingency, and the transaction shall in all respects proceed as an all‑cash purchase from Seller's perspective.

6.3 **No Escape Hatch.** Buyer's inability or failure to obtain financing, an unsatisfactory or low appraisal, or any condition or requirement imposed by Buyer's lender shall **not** excuse Buyer's performance, extend any deadline, or entitle Buyer to terminate the Contract or to the return of the Deposit. Consistent with Section 4 (Sufficient Assets) of the Contract and Buyer's full appraisal waiver in Section 43, Buyer represents that Buyer has, and will have at Closing, sufficient liquid funds to complete the purchase without financing, and shall provide reasonable proof of funds upon Seller's request. Any failure of Buyer to close for a financing‑related reason is a default by Buyer under Section 27 of the Contract, and the Deposit shall be paid over to Seller as provided therein. *(See Drafting Note 2 regarding Buyer's lender and Seller's post‑Closing occupancy.)*
