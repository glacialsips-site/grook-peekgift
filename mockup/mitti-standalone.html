Our studio

One potter, one wheel, a very patient kiln.

I learned to throw on my grandmother's veranda in Jaipur, watching the local kumhar turn river clay into something that held water and held memory. Mitti is that, transplanted to a garage studio in New Jersey.

Every piece is thrown, trimmed, bisque-fired, glazed and fired again — by hand, by me. Which is why no two are quite alike, and why I wouldn't have it any other way.

— Ananya
